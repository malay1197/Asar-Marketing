Local Business Footfall & Visibility Blueprint

THE CHALLENGE

A premium dining destination had high customer satisfaction but inconsistent weekday footfall and low social media discovery.

THE STRATEGY

Reposition the restaurant's social presence from static food photography to engaging short-form reels focusing on dining experience and chef artistry.

THE EXECUTION

12 monthly high-definition Reels, targeted hyper-local Instagram ad campaigns within 8km radius, and Google Business Profile optimization.

CASE STUDY ARCHITECTURE // CONCEPT CONCEPT CASE STUDY

High-Intent Lead Generation Engine

THE CHALLENGE

A specialized fitness studio needed consistent monthly qualified leads without relying on low-quality automated lead forms.

THE STRATEGY

Design a 2-step direct WhatsApp lead funnel combined with Meta offer ads highlighting personalized consultation sessions.

THE EXECUTION

High-converting Meta video ad creative + instant WhatsApp pre-filled qualifier link to filter high-intent prospects immediately.
